## 2. Why this product (A)

- **The buyer's problem is real and voiced (FACT, gyakorikerdesek):** "Buying a specific gift for an angler is a big risk… everyone has a different method… so a voucher is the safest."
- **Current solution: a voucher or a generic mug.** Both are low-emotion. Tackle-shop gift packs assume the buyer knows the angler's method.
- **The gap (INFERENCE):** a box sold to the *buyer*, built around:
  - a 3-question method quiz;
  - a "swap if it doesn't fit his method" guarantee;
  - a Hungarian "what's inside" card;
  - a delivery-by-Christmas promise.
- **Proven willingness to pay:** branded advent calendars and gift crates already sell at 18–60k Ft in Hungary (FACT). The price point is not invented.
- **The gift buyer compares on reassurance, not unit price (INFERENCE).** That is the main defence against Temu.
- **Timing:** it is 7 October, so the €100 test can run before the Q4 peak and the outcome is known by mid-November.
- **Inventory risk can be deferred:**
  - Test 1 needs no stock.
  - Test 2 needs about 15 boxes.

## 3. Why Hungary

- Over 1M registered anglers (MOHOSZ, FACT). Feeder fishing is "increasingly popular" (FACT, qualitative).
- The category is Hungarian-specific: methods, brands (Carp Zoom, Haldorádó) and lake culture. A Hungarian-language quiz and card matter.
- Locker delivery (about 10k lockers, 41% of deliveries) suits a 1 kg, non-fragile box.
- Alanyi adómentesség (up to 20M Ft in 2026) is what makes the economics work at this scale (FACT, NAV).
- The EU €3 duty per item from July 2026 slightly raises cross-border gift-box prices (FACT).

## 4. Customer

**Kata, 34, Pest county.**
- Partner Gábor, 37, feeder-fishes at a lake most weekends.
- She doesn't know feeder from boilie fishing.
- Budget 15–30k Ft; buys from Facebook ads.
- Pays by card or card-on-delivery; wants the parcel in a locker by 20 December.

**Secondary buyers:**
- Adult children buying for a father.
- Mothers buying for teen sons. Avoid positioning the box *for children*, because a children's kit is a toy under EN71.

**Threat:** tackle chains have wholesale prices and audiences, and could copy the quiz and guarantee within a season. The defence is speed and buyer-side positioning, not a structural moat.

## 7. Temu / AliExpress risk

- **Can Temu sell the same box?** It can sell the terminal tackle and generic tools, which is why those parts alone are not the product.
- **What Temu cannot easily sell:**
  - Hungarian branded consumables
  - Method-specific Hungarian curation
  - A Hungarian card
  - Engraving
  - A swap guarantee
  - Guaranteed pre-Christmas locker delivery
- **Moat rating: WEAK–MODERATE.** It depends on buyer psychology (reassurance), not on product uniqueness.
- **Danger:** if the contents look like Temu tackle, the gift embarrasses the buyer and the brand dies on reviews. **Product credibility is the single biggest risk.**

## 12. Compliance and logistics

**Compliance (ESTIMATE / UNVERIFIED unless marked):**
- **Bait and groundbait:** buy finished, labelled Hungarian-brand products only, and never repack them. Feed-law status of repacked bait is UNVERIFIED.
- **No blades.** Use **lead-free** weights (the EU lead restriction for fishing tackle is UNVERIFIED for 2026).
- **Personalised parts:** exempt from withdrawal (FACT, 45/2014 §29(1)(c)). Everything else: 14-day withdrawal right, and the 2-year legal warranty applies.
- **Packaging EPR (MOHU):** registration is required (FACT).
- **Labelling:** Hungarian-language labels and the card.
- **Positioning:** do **not** market the box as a children's kit (toy rules).

**Logistics:**
- About 1 kg, non-fragile, locker-friendly; shipping about 1,000–1,500 Ft.
- Pack by hand: about 15 minutes per box.
- Bait expiry: buy stock in small batches.

## 14. Validation plan

Each stage is gated. If any kill criterion triggers, stop.

**Before spending (€0, this week):**
- **Competitor check:** run Meta Ad Library (HU) on "horgász ajándék". If at least 2 advertisers already run a quiz-plus-swap-guarantee offer for non-anglers, re-score.
- **Search volume:** check Google Keyword Planner volumes.
- **Sourcing:**
  - Request wholesale quotes from 2–3 Hungarian tackle distributors.
  - **Kill if** a core box an angler would call "useful" lands above 10,000 Ft.
  - **Kill if** distributors won't sell to a new non-shop seller.
- **Angler panel:** show a box mock-up to 3–5 real anglers. **Kill if** most call it junk.

**Test 1: €100 (about 20–24 October, 7 days).** Landing page with the quiz and real preorder at checkout ("ships from 25 November", refund if not fulfilled). Two angles, broad women 28–60.

Pass criteria:
- Link CTR ≥ 1.2%
- CPC ≤ 250 Ft
- Quiz completion ≥ 30%
- At least 4 paid preorders (implied CAC ≤ about 9,000 Ft)

**Test 2: €200 (about 1–15 November).** Buy about 15 boxes of stock (about €350 of COGS; this is outside the ad budget). Ship the preorders and gather reviews and UGC.

Pass criteria:
- CAC ≤ 10,000 Ft over at least 10 orders
- Swap or refund rate ≤ 15%
- No "junk" reviews

**Test 3: €300 (about 20 November – 14 December, Christmas peak).** Stock is capped at about 40 units.

Pass criteria:
- CAC ≤ 10,000 Ft
- At least 25 orders
- Stock sell-through ≥ 70% by 16 December

**Total worst-case exposure:** about €600 of ads, about €350 of stock and a few months of fixed costs. That is **≤ about €1,000**.

## 15. Kill criteria (summary)

- Landed COGS for a credible core box is above 10,000 Ft, or the angler panel calls it junk.
- Test 1:
  - CTR is below 0.8%, or
  - CPC is above 350 Ft, or
  - there are fewer than 2 preorders on €100.
- Any test: CAC above 12,000 Ft across at least 10 orders. That is the break-even with buffer while VAT-exempt.
- Swap or refund rate above 15%, or any "embarrassing gift" review pattern.
- The seller is, or must become, VAT-registered (27%). Break-even CAC drops to 7,887 Ft, so stop.
- After 16 December: no new stock. Liquidate leftovers in January rather than rebuying.
