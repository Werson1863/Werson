# Learn-to-crochet starter kit with German video lessons, launched in Germany and Austria

**Investment memo · 7 October 2026 · Shopify One-Product Research Engine v1.0**

**Verdict: CONDITIONAL GO.** With my own money I would test this product, but only as a capped test of about €5,000 in Germany and Austria. That covers the first inventory, setup and €1,500 of ads, with written kill rules. I would not commit €10–20k up front. The realistic case loses money in November, is about break-even in December and makes a small profit (~€600) in January. €2,000/month within 90 days happens only in the upside case.

---

## 1. Executive summary

I screened 76 physical products, scored 22 seriously, cut to 10 and then to 3 finalists. The best opportunity is a **premium beginner amigurumi crochet kit with step-by-step German video lessons**, sold first in Germany and Austria (later Switzerland), as "the Woobles for German speakers". The evidence for demand is broad:
- 85% of German women do handicrafts, and 18–29-year-olds are now the largest group.
- Knitting and crochet yarn is the biggest segment of the German handicraft market, at €370M.
- #crochet has billions of TikTok views.
- The Woobles shows the model works at scale: an estimated 6–6.5% conversion rate and $50–75 AOV.

The competition is weak where it matters. Cheap Amazon kits (€12–20) get the same complaints over and over: unclear or machine-translated instructions, too little yarn, and "beginner" kits that aren't for beginners. No dominant German-language premium kit brand turned up.

The weakness is unit economics. At a €48 AOV, contribution before ads is about **€25 per order**, so CPA has to fall to about €22 by month 3 for the store to make money. That is plausible but unproven. A Spanish knitting-kit brand, We Are Knitters (€14.7M revenue in 2023), went insolvent in 2025, which is a reminder that this category can lose money at scale.

The runner-up, an **acupressure "wind-down" mat set**, has better per-order margins (about €29–30). It loses on competition: mass retail (Rossmann, Albert Heijn, Decathlon, Tchibo) sells mats for €20–40, and German advertising law (HWG) bans its strongest selling claim, pain relief. #3, a **54 mm espresso workflow kit**, is a commodity niche and does not reach profit in my realistic case.

- Raw notes, the candidate database, the scoring and the calculator are in `research_notes/Shopify one-product research/` (`00`–`06` + `unit_economics.py`).

**Five checks to run yourself on day 1, because I couldn't** (each takes 5–20 minutes):
1. Google Trends, Germany, 5 years: "häkeln", "häkelset", "amigurumi", "akupressurmatte".
2. Meta Ad Library, Germany: "Häkelset", "Häkeln lernen", "amigurumi". Count advertisers and note ads running for 60+ days.

If check 2 shows 10 or more German advertisers with long-running "Häkelset" ads, competition is stronger than I found. In that case, cut the Competition score by 3 points.

## 5. Winner: detailed analysis

### 5.1 Product
- **What:** a gift-ready box containing:
  - one original, cute amigurumi character in easy-to-see chunky chenille yarn
  - an ergonomic hook, stitch markers, safety eyes, stuffing and a needle
  - a QR card that opens a **German video course**: one short close-up clip per round, slow, with a left-handed version
- **Brand promise:** *"Dein erstes Häkeltier an einem Abend — oder wir helfen dir persönlich."* ("Your first crochet animal in one evening, or we'll help you personally.")
- **Category:** hobbies / crafts (DIY kit for adults).
- **Target customer:** women aged 18–45 in DACH who want a calm, screen-free hobby, plus gift buyers (partners, mothers, friends). Secondary: parents crocheting *with* teens aged 14+.

### 5.2 Demand evidence (classification: **STRONG**)
- **FACT** (Initiative Handarbeit via [t-online](https://www.t-online.de/leben/aktuelles/id_100586036/stricken-und-haekeln-trendet-auf-social-media-das-ist-der-grund.html) and [Rheinpfalz](https://www.rheinpfalz.de/lokal/karlsruhe_artikel,-handarbeiten-bei-karlsruher-messe-im-blickpunkt-_arid,5643108.html)):
  - 85% of women in Germany do handicrafts, up from 76% in 2021.
  - 18–29-year-olds are now the largest group.
  - Knitting and crochet yarn is the biggest single segment at **€370M**, in a ~€1B German handicraft market.
- **FACT:** Hobbycraft UK sold more than 500,000 balls of wool over Christmas 2025, and beginner kits were up 32% ([Retail Gazette](https://www.retailgazette.co.uk/blog/2026/01/hobbycraft-sales-handmade/)).
- **FACT:** Guided craft-kit sales are up 86% year on year, and searches for portable hobbies are up 136% in six months (US data, Michaels; [AOL](https://www.aol.com/lifestyle/8-biggest-crafting-trends-2026-194606392.html)).
- **FACT** (third-party estimates): on Amazon US, the Woobles beginner kit sells ~2k/month and the cheapest hook-set "kit" ~7k/month ([asinsight](https://www.asinsight.com/report/US/beginner-crochet-kit-for-adults)).
- **Search volume in Germany: unverified.** Check Google Trends and Amazon.de.

### 5.3 Trend evidence (sustained, not viral)
- #crochet: ~14–24B TikTok views; crochet is the most popular craft on TikTok ([gathered.how](https://gathered.how/knitting-and-crochet/crochet/tiktok-viewers-hooked-craft), [Daily Dot](https://www.dailydot.com/culture/giant-crocheted-earthworm-gen-z)).
- A May 2025 survey of 1,600 Americans aged 18–28 found that most have at least one "grandma hobby", with crochet near the top ([prismnews](https://www.prismnews.com/hobbies/crocheting/gen-z-swaps-nightlife-for-crocheting-embracing-slow-and)).
- Seasonality (**INFERENCE** from search snippets): interest rises in Q4 and peaks around January, then dips in summer. Launching in October/November catches both Christmas gifting and New Year "new hobby" demand. Summer will be the hard months.
- This is **not** a short-lived meme. The German hobby data shows a permanent post-pandemic level, without the 2020–21 spike.

### 5.5 Customer complaint mining → product spec

## 9. Go-to-market plan: first practical steps

**Week 1 (by 14 Oct)**
1. Run the five verification checks in section 2. If the Meta Ad Library shows a strong German premium competitor, stop and re-score.
2. Order samples: 2 Alibaba kit OEMs, 1 Turkish chenille yarn source, 1 EU option (Hardicraft/Faire). Test the yarn yourself: does it split, is it visible on camera, can a beginner finish?
3. Register the business (Gewerbe) if needed, register in LUCID with a dual-system licence, and buy the legal-text package.

**Weeks 2–3**
4. Design 2 original characters (no Woobles look-alikes) plus 1 Christmas ornament. Film the course: a one-clip-per-round structure, plus a left-handed version.
5. Build the German Shopify store with the product page above (guarantees, video preview, "fertig an einem Abend"). Bundle ladder: €34.90 / €64.90; free shipping from €39.
6. Labels: "Bastelset für Jugendliche ab 14 Jahren und Erwachsene — kein Spielzeug. Enthält verschluckbare Kleinteile." ("Craft kit for ages 14+ and adults — not a toy. Contains small parts that can be swallowed.") Market the kit to adults; the EU Toy Safety Directive covers products for play by children under 14 ([law.resource.org guidance](https://law.resource.org/pub/eu/toys/guidance/ce_toys_en.pdf)). Keep EN 71-3 certificates for the eyes.
7. Order 300 starter kits + 150 refills **by air**. Since **1 July 2026 the EU charges €3 per item category on parcels under €150** ([PwC](https://www.pwc.dk/en/articles-publications/2026/eu-abolishes-the-150-low-value-customs-duty-threshold-from-1-july-2026.html)), so ship stock in bulk to an EU warehouse or your own packing table; do not ship each order from China. If you work from Hungary, pack the first ~300 orders yourself and compare GLS/Packeta Hungary→Germany rates with a German or Polish 3PL (€5–9 all-in per order; [eightx](https://eightx.co/blog/germany-3pl-cost-index)).

**Week 4 (~1 Nov): launch**
8. Meta: €50/day, Advantage+ shopping campaign plus a 3-angle ABO test, 12 creatives. TikTok: organic daily posts plus Spark Ads on the winners.
9. Klaviyo flows: welcome, abandoned cart, day-5 "next character", day-21 refill.

**Kill / scale rules (write them down before spending)**
- After **€1,200–1,500 spend and ≥ 12 creatives tested**: if blended CPA > **€35** *and* link CTR < 1.0% → **stop**. Sell the stock and keep the remaining capital.
- CPA €25–35 → continue through Christmas only if AOV ≥ €45; reassess on 5 January.
- CPA ≤ **€22** with AOV ≥ €45 → scale the budget 20–30% per week.
- Fallback: if crochet fails *on creative response* (CTR < 0.8%), the runner-up acupressure set is the next test. Its samples cost ~€100, and suppliers offer MOQs from 100 sets at $8.30–9 per set ([sourcing listing](https://sourcing-sb.ship4wd.com/product/acupuncture-massage-mat-acupuncture-yoga-mat-acupuncture-massage-mat-exercise-mat-acupuncture-pillow-yoga-acupuncture-mat)).

## 10. Main risks: why this could fail

1. **CPA never gets below ~€25 at a ~€45 AOV.** First-order economics are thin. *What I checked:* the Woobles achieves an estimated 6–6.5% conversion and $50–75 AOV, so the model can work, but a new store will not start there. *Mitigation:* lead with the trio and refills, set free shipping at €39, kill at €35 CPA.
2. **The category can collapse at scale.** *What I checked:* We Are Knitters went from €14.9M peak revenue and €1.6M profit (2021) to €1.65M accumulated losses, a prepack sale for €914k (June 2025) and liquidation (October 2025), after demand normalised and logistics and raw-material costs rose ([techleap](https://finder.techleap.nl/news/feed/we-are-knitters-sold-for-914k), [FashionUnited](https://fashionunited.es/noticias/empresas/we-are-knitters-entra-en-liquidacion-tras-la-resolucion-de-su-concurso-de-acreedores/2025101647352)). *Lesson:* keep fixed costs near zero, don't stock for a pandemic-level peak, and grow on reorders rather than only on paid acquisition.
3. **The "German video" edge can be copied.** *What I checked:* Amazon sellers (ElysiumEq and others) already sell kits with German step-by-step videos for €12–20 ([which.one](https://de.which.one/p/B0FKH2266N), [Völkner](https://www.voelkner.de/products/13197104/Haekelset-Tiere-fuer-Anfaenger-mit-Garn-und-Video-3er-Set.html)), and the Woobles could localise. *Mitigation:* compete on original design, the pre-started round, the yarn and help guarantees, and community. Register the brand.
4. **Seasonality and the summer dip.** Q4 ad costs are high in November, January is strong, and May–August is likely weak (INFERENCE from search-trend snippets; check Google Trends). *Mitigation:* order inventory in small batches; add summer projects (crochet bags, tops); accept lower ad spend in summer.
5. **Compliance and product safety.** If the kit is marketed for children, the finished animal falls under toy law; packaging must be registered in LUCID (personal registration by 12 Aug 2026); GPSR product information is required. *Mitigation:* market it for ages 14+ and adults, add the warning label, keep EN 71-3 certificates, and register before the first sale.

Smaller risks: yarn colour batches vary (photograph each batch); filming takes many owner hours; Meta account bans on new ad accounts (warm the account up, keep a backup payment method).

## 11. Why not #2 or #3

**#2 Acupressure "wind-down" set (score 66).** I would test it, but second.
- *Pros:* proven single-product DTC business. Shakti Mat US makes an estimated ~$1.1M per month with 121k visitors ([BrandSearch](https://brandsearch.co/brands/shaktimat.com)); Shakti Mat Europe (NL) about €89k per month ([BrandSearch](https://brandsearch.co/brands/shaktimat.eu)). Exploding Topics shows 33.1k searches, +16% ([ET](https://explodingtopics.com/topic/acupressure-mat)). There is a tailwind from the "nervous system regulation" trend ([heidelberg24](https://www.heidelberg24.de/verbraucher/die-besten-nervous-system-regulation-tricks-von-tiktok-zr-94474879.html)). Mats are a known Christmas gift (named Swedish "Christmas gift of the year" in 2009; [Sveriges Radio](https://www.sverigesradio.se/artikel/3234382)). Wholesale is $3.85–9 at MOQ 100, and contribution is about €29 per order.
- *Cons:*
  - **Mass retail anchors the price at €20–40:** Rossmann, Albert Heijn/LifeGoods, Decathlon (497 reviews, 80.5% five-star, so quality is hard to beat), Tchibo, and Hungarian shops at 8,400–12,400 Ft ([Decathlon](https://www.decathlon.ch/de/r/akupressurmatte-und-kissen-blau/343121/c251c309c344m8767817), [AH](https://www.ah.nl/producten/product/wi600627), [Rossmann](https://www.rossmann.de/de/ideenwelt-ideenwelt-akupressur-matte/p/4068134185068)).
  - **No pain-relief claims under German advertising law (HWG):** a German law firm warns that ads promising relief from back pain draw warning letters ([IT-Recht Kanzlei](https://www.it-recht-kanzlei.de/achtung-abmahnung-irrefuehrung-werbung-schlafprodukte.html)).
  - **One-off purchase, few reorders,** and returned mats often can't be resold because they touch the body.
- *Realistic 90 days:* −€3.6k. Upside: +€1.5k.

**#3 54 mm espresso workflow kit (score 61).** I would not put money into this.
- The hobby is real: WDT tools are the fastest-growing espresso accessory on Amazon US ([asinsight](https://www.asinsight.com/report/US/espresso-accessories-58mm)).
- But German shops already sell WDT + puck screen + dosing ring sets for **€18.99–29.44** ([preissuchmaschine](https://www.preissuchmaschine.de/trend/ESPR/Espresso-verteiler.html?mehr=15)), Germany is mainly a fully-automatic-machine market (De'Longhi Magnifica S #1; [leinetal24](https://www.leinetal24.de/produktempfehlung/die-meistverkauften-delonghikaffeemaschinen-in-deutschland-zr-94220032.html)), and enthusiasts say the stock tamper is "fine" and buy on spec.
- *Realistic 90 days:* −€4.6k. Even the upside case doesn't break even.

## 12. Final verdict

**CONDITIONAL GO**, for a beginner amigurumi crochet kit with a German video course, launched in Germany and Austria at €34.90 (starter) and €64.90 (trio).

The conditions:
1. The day-1 checks in section 2 (Meta Ad Library, Google Trends, Amazon.de) don't show an entrenched German premium competitor.
2. A sample passes a real-beginner test: a non-crocheter finishes the animal in one evening using only the videos.
3. You cap the first test at about €5,200 total, with €1,500 of ads, and follow the kill rule (CPA above €35 after €1,200–1,500 → stop).

**"If this were my own money, would I launch this Shopify store?"** Yes, but as a €5k test with a hard stop, not a €20k bet. It has real, durable demand. The competition is cheap and gets bad reviews. It ships in a €4 parcel, is rarely returned and has a natural reorder product. The realistic outcome is a small loss over the first 90 days and roughly €500/month profit by January, with €1,000–2,000/month only if the ads perform at the upside level. I would not launch any of the other 75 candidates first.
