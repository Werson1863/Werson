## The criterion that sinks everything: €500/month in the realistic case

Even a product that clears criteria 1–7 and 9–13 can't show evidence for criterion 8.

**What the Hungarian benchmarks say** (agency blogs, Tier 3): Meta CPA is 3,500–25,000 Ft, and average Hungarian webshop ROAS is 3–5×. Those figures come from established, managed accounts. I found no evidence on new-store, cold-traffic ROAS in Hungary.

**What it takes.** At a 25,000 Ft AOV with ~€43 contribution per order (VAT-exempt), €500 net in month 3 needs roughly:
- **3× cold-traffic ROAS:** about 30 orders on ~€700 of ads.
- **2× ROAS:** 75 orders on ~€2,600 of ads.

With 27% VAT, 3× ROAS needs 86 orders on ~€2,000 of ads. New stores typically start around 1–2× (INFERENCE). So €500/month appears only when you assume a ≥3× ROAS that nothing supports. That is the upside case, which your rules exclude.

The other blocker is evidence access. The data that could *prove* a candidate here was blocked from this environment:
- Meta Ad Library (do Hungarian advertisers run the same product for 60+ days?)
- Google Keyword Planner / Trends (Hungarian search volume)
- Company filings on e-beszamolo.im.gov.hu (do small Hungarian competitors make money?)

## How a real GO could be found (free, done by you, about 2 hours per candidate)

A candidate earns GO only if all three checks pass:
1. **Meta Ad Library, Hungary:** at least 3 Hungarian advertisers running ads for the product for **60+ days**. That is evidence their acquisition pays.
2. **e-beszamolo.im.gov.hu:** at least one small Hungarian competitor whose **revenue is growing year on year** while the market stays fragmented.
3. **Google Keyword Planner, Hungary:** **≥ 1,000 searches/month** for the Hungarian product term, with no dominant brand on page one.

The candidates closest to passing are worth checking this way, though none is recommended today:
- **Gift packs for anglers:** over 1M registered Hungarian anglers; no gift-pack seller found.
- **A Hungarian-guided puppy starter bundle.**
- **A firewood-heating efficiency bundle:** 1.3M households heat with wood; the moat is the Hungarian content.

If one passes all three checks, it can be re-scored against the hard criteria.
