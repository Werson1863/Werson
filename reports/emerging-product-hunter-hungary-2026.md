## Winner: why this product

- **Proven abroad, absent in Hungary.**
  - Brick.me sells from £49.99 / $59.99 and has 4.8★ from **1,339 Trustpilot reviews**.
  - BrickPicFun runs a Dutch workshop with free EU shipping.
  - LEGO sells an official Mosaic Maker (40179) at ~€102 in the EU.
  - Hungarian search turned up only *printed* photo bricks (fotodoboz.hu) and LEGO-style *drawings* (egyediportrek.hu). No build-it-yourself kit maker was found.
- **Temu can't easily copy it.** Each kit is made to order from the customer's photo; Chinese custom sellers take 2–3 weeks and now pay the EU's €3 low-value-parcel duty. A local maker ships in 3–5 days.
- **Personalised goods have no 14-day EU withdrawal right**, so return risk is low. A 3% remake allowance is budgeted for missing pieces.
- **The video sells itself.** Photo → 30-second time-lapse build → wall reveal → recipient's reaction. That is the problem → product → result structure in under 20 seconds.
- **Hungary's gift calendar is dense:** Mikulás, Christmas, Valentine's, name days (névnap), Mother's Day, ballagás (graduation), weddings, grandparents.
- **Margins are strong:** a 32×32 kit costs ~€10.5 landed and a 48×48 ~€21 (ESTIMATE, no supplier quote), against 19,990–29,990 Ft prices.
- **Low capital and you control quality.** Bricks are bought in bulk by colour and counted per order by weight, which fixes the category's known complaints (missing pieces, wrong colours).
- **Clear expansion path:** pet portraits, couples' "build night" duo, wedding guest-book mosaics (B2B; Australian vendors charge from $940), corporate gifts.

## Why now

- **Adults buying toys for themselves is structural.** Circana puts European "kidult" toy spending at $4.8B, **28.5% of the toy market**, with ~40% of European toy buyers buying for adults. LEGO has built a whole adult line.
- **Screen-free, hands-on hobbies are rising** (junk journaling, crochet, mail clubs; CNN, YPulse 2025–26). Building a mosaic fits "a calm evening off the phone".
- **Cheap Chinese parcels have become more expensive.** The EU's €3 per-item duty on low-value parcels started 1 July 2026, and Hungarian press reports a sharp drop in Chinese parcel numbers after Temu's 2025 peak (2.1M Hungarian buyers). Local made-to-order products gain ground.

## Why Hungary

- **No local competitor found.** Foreign suppliers ship from the UK, the Netherlands or China, with English interfaces and slower delivery.
- **Photo gifts are already a habit.** Canvas prints of the grandchildren are a popular grandparent gift, Venus Puzzle has sold photo puzzles since 2004, and diamond painting fills 21+ pages on Árgép. Hungarians already understand "turn my photo into a DIY craft".
- **Ads should be cheaper than in the West.** Meta CPM benchmarks are $5.50 for Poland and $6.20 for Czechia. Hungary isn't listed; I assumed €4.5–7.
- **The VAT exemption changes the economics.** A Hungarian small business is VAT-exempt up to **20M Ft of revenue in 2026 (22M in 2027)** (NAV). That lifts contribution from €32 to €47 per order. **Check with an accountant whether a new business can elect this at registration.**

## Customer

- **Primary:** a woman aged **28–45**, in a relationship and/or a mother of young children, on Facebook and Instagram daily.
  - **Situation:** buys most of the family's gifts.
  - **Problem:** "every year the same canvas or mug; I want something personal they'll remember".
  - **Motivation:** a unique, shared experience that ends up on the wall.
  - **Buying trigger:** the Christmas deadline, an anniversary, Valentine's, a name day, a gift for the grandparents.
  - **Objections:** price; "is it hard or time-consuming?"; "will my photo look good?"; "will it arrive in time?"; reluctance to prepay (about half of Hungarians still prefer cash on delivery).
- **Secondary:** couples wanting a "date-night build", pet owners (pet portrait), and adult LEGO fans.

## Acquisition

1. **Meta Ads (Facebook/Instagram Reels):** the main paid channel for women aged 28–55. Broad targeting and a sales objective, about €15–25/day.
2. **TikTok / Reels organic plus Spark Ads:** one time-lapse a day. Organic reach is the cheapest source of early proof.
3. **UGC / micro-creators:** mums, couples and pet accounts get a free kit in exchange for a build video. Later, wedding planners (B2B).

**Strongest creative angle:** split screen of the photo, then a 30-second time-lapse, then the wall reveal and the gift recipient's reaction ("Karácsonyi reakció": Christmas reaction). Second angle: a pet portrait.

## Would you invest €1,000?

**YES**, as a capped test, not a launch.

- **Downside:** if the kill rules are followed, about **€600–700** at most (€350 of ads plus setup and leftover stock).
- **Realistic case:** roughly break-even to **+€900** in 90 days, depending on VAT status.
- **Upside:** **€6–11k in 90 days**, with repeat gift occasions all year and a B2B wedding line.

That is the asymmetric shape you asked for: limited downside, real upside, little local competition, strong content, high margin.

What could make it fail:
- Hungarian demand is **completely unverified**.
- The economics rest on assumed ad costs.
- Production is manual and made to order.
- A Hungarian photo-gift shop could copy the idea.

**I would not put a forint more than €1,000 into it until the November test shows CPA ≤ €25.**
