## 3. Facts that cut against the previous report
- LEGO Mosaic Maker **is** sold in Hungary (44,990 Ft) — the previous memo said "Hungarian availability unclear".
- Build time: LEGO 40179 (48×48) ≈ **10–13 hours** / "three evenings" (Brickfanatics review extract). 32×32 ≈ 4–6 hours (ESTIMATE, ~44% of pieces).
- Cheaper substitutes deliver "my photo on the wall" in 2 days for 9,300–15,900 Ft with COD.
- Pixelhobby already serves "build my own photo" (mostly for kids).

## 4. Facts in favour
- Hungarians pay 9,000–65,000 Ft for personalised pet portraits — many sellers, so the segment pays.
- Wholesale bricks: 1x1 round plates $13.50–15/kg, MOQ 2 kg (Alibaba, DELO TOYS) → COGS far below the previous estimate.
- Price gap: LEGO's version is 44,990 Ft (5 colours, one size) → room for 19,990–29,990 Ft.
- LEGO H1-2026 net profit ~€1.15bn, +32% (Euronews) — adult building is structurally growing.

## 5. Legal / tax (authoritative where possible)
- Withdrawal: Govt. Decree 45/2014 (II.26.) §29(1)(c) — no withdrawal right for non-prefabricated goods made to the consumer's
  instructions or clearly personalised (text per [njt.jog.gov.hu](https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/2014-45-20-22); the search extract
  did not show the full paragraph → verify). Residual risk: the kit consists of standard bricks; only the plan is personalised.
  **Warranty (kellékszavatosság, Ptk. 6:159 ff.) still applies** → missing/wrong pieces must be replaced.
- VAT: alanyi adómentesség threshold 20M Ft (2026), 22M (2027) ([NAV](https://nav.gov.hu/sajtoszoba/hirek/Jo_hir_a_kkv-knak_tovabb_emelkedik_az_alanyi_adomentesseg_hatara));
  a NEW business can elect it at registration if expected revenue is below the threshold ([Infostart/NAV](https://infostart.hu/gazdasag/2013/01/08/nav-alanyi-mentesseg-katasoknak-ev-kozben-is-539370)).
  Exempt sellers cannot reclaim input/import VAT. Income tax (KATA / átalányadó) is NOT in the model.

## 6. Payment & trust
- Prepaid ≈ 50% of Hungarian orders, COD falling; card-on-delivery 22% → 31%; lockers 41% (PwC via Economx).
- Key insight: bricks are standard and reusable → a refused COD parcel costs only shipping + labour + printed guide (~3,500 Ft).
  COD after preview approval is therefore viable and probably worth offering.
- Trust needed before paid traffic: company details/Impressum, Hungarian phone/e-mail, real sample photos and build videos,
  ≥ 5–10 reviews (seed with friends/family at cost), Barion/SimplePay, Foxpost/Packeta, free preview before payment, replacement guarantee.
