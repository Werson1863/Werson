**Temu risk: very high.** The core part (the fan) is an identical commodity sold cross-border for under €15. The buyer group (rural households heating with wood) skews price-sensitive (INFERENCE). Sales are seasonal (October–February).

The bundle is also electrical (thermoelectric fan) and the meter is battery-powered. Importing them directly would trigger:
- EEE/battery EPR registration with MOHU
- possibly a CE/LVD/EMC documentation check

These are UNVERIFIED compliance costs.
