## Customer avatar
- **Primary (launch Oct–Dec): the gift buyer.** Women 25–55 buying for a friend, sister, daughter or partner. Evidence: competitors all sell gift boxes and vouchers (14, 16); Amazon titles stress "Geschenk"; Hobbycraft Christmas peak (UK); desired.de gift articles (21). INFERENCE: Q4 timing favours gifting.
- **Secondary (January): the self-buying beginner.** Women 18–35 looking for a calm, phone-free hobby. Evidence: Initiative Handarbeit (18–29 largest group, 38); mindfulness/phone-free motive (28); competitor copy "kreative Pause / Auszeit vom Bildschirm" (14, 16).
