## Why the winner wins (and where it does not)
- At **equal CPA** the acupressure set has better per-order economics (contribution ≈ €29–30 vs €25–27). The crochet kit wins on
  competition quality, differentiation, creative variety and repeat purchase — **not** on first-order margin. The case for it
  rests on reaching a lower CPA (impulse price, very visual product, Woobles-type conversion) and on reorders. If the ad test
  shows crochet CPA ≥ acupressure CPA, the ranking flips; that is the main test of the 30-day phase.
