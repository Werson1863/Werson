# Method and limits (Shopify One-Product Research Engine v1.0)

Research date: 2026-10-07.

## Tool access (FACT)
- The session's egress proxy blocked **every direct page fetch** tried: amazon.de, tiktok.com, reddit.com (incl. old.reddit, pullpush API),
  trends.google.com, facebook.com/ads/library, alibaba.com, aliexpress.com, etsy.com, pinterest.com, faire.com, cjdropshipping.com,
  idealo.de, bol.com, emag.hu, arukereso.hu, trustpilot.com, and secondary blogs (lengow, fastmoss).
- Only the web search tool worked. **All evidence = search-engine result extracts** (titles, URLs, snippet summaries).
- Consequence: no Google Trends curve, no Meta Ad Library count, no TikTok view count, no Reddit thread and no supplier page was
  opened directly. Where a claim relies on a snippet it is labelled; anything not seen is `unverified`.

## Labels
- FACT = stated by a first-party or named source in a search extract (URL given).
- ESTIMATE = my calculation or assumption (inputs shown).
- INFERENCE = reasoning from facts.
- unverified = not found / could not be opened.

## Third-party data caveats
- asinsight.com / ecomcrew / salehoo "monthly sales" = Amazon **US** estimates from third-party tools, not audited.
- Exploding Topics volumes = their own US-centric estimates.
- Kalodata / FastMoss TikTok GMV = estimates.
