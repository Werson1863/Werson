## Filtered shower head (DE/AT/HU hard water)
- DTC proof FACT: Jolie (US) $4M yr1 (2022) → $28M (2023) → ~$40M (2024), filter subscription every ~90 days, 90% DTC. [Modern Retail](https://www.modernretail.co/marketing/dtc-briefing-how-jolie-reached-4-2m-in-sales-its-first-year-while-staying-profitable/), [Beauty Independent](https://beautyindependent.com/filtered-showerhead-brand-jolie-ulta-beauty)
- DE: many "Kalk Duschfilter 2026 Modell" on eBay; Philips sells a shower filter (AWP1775). [Philips](https://www.philips.de/c-p/AWP1775_10/duschfilter)
- **Killer issue FACT**: "No 'Kalkfilter' in the mechanical sense removes dissolved hardness minerals — only certified softening systems" (bau.de expert forum). KDF/vitamin-C filters address chlorine, not calcium. Germany's main pain = Kalk → product cannot honestly deliver the promise customers want → refund and UWG risk. Stiftung Warentest jug-filter test headline: "Überflüssig bis schädlich". [bau.de](https://bau.de/forum/trinkwasser/10338.php), [test.de](https://www.test.de/Wasserfilter-im-Test-Gut-filtert-keiner-4840828-4841731/)
- Complaints FACT: lower pressure, leaks after cartridge change, cartridges last 1 month, heavy head sags. [kimola](https://kimola.com/reports/unlock-shower-bliss-with-our-filtered-shower-head-report-amazon-en-us-152510)

## Weighted vest (women, walking)
- Demand FACT: Amazon "weighted vest" searches +285% YoY, 6 of top-10 terms include "woman" (Athletech). US sales +50% to $27M.
- DE price anchor FACT: **Aldi Nord/Süd Crane 4 kg vest €14.99** (mydealz, Jan 2025); eBay 5 kg €24.99, 10 kg €61.99; Kaufland AT 10 kg €72.20. [mydealz](https://www.mydealz.de/deals/crane-gewichtsweste-4-kg-schwarz-ab-donnerstsg-in-den-aldi-nord-filialen-2505155)
- INFERENCE: 4–10 kg parcels → €6–12 shipping in EU, inbound freight heavy; sizing/fit returns; discounter anchors. **Rejected** (logistics + price anchor).

## Visual timer (ADHD)
- Time Timer DE €32–40; eBay generic 60-min visual timers widespread. ET "ADHD timer" +1320% (small US volume 170). → AOV low (€25–35), easy to clone, single SKU. Penalised.

## Matcha whisk set
- Chasen complaints FACT: tines break within weeks, mould, bamboo splinters in drink. Price €25 (SE whisk), ~€42 eBay set. Food-contact materials regulation (EU 1935/2004) applies; matcha itself = food (LMIV labelling). Penalised.
