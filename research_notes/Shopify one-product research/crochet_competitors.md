# Crochet kit — German/Austrian competitor map (search-extract evidence; see crochet_sources.md)

**Correction to the first report:** it said "no German premium DTC competitor found". That is **wrong**. At least two
German-language DTC brands sell exactly the planned concept, a large French DIY brand sells it in German, and The Woobles
sells on Amazon.de with a German listing.

## What this means
- **Concept is validated as sellable in DE** (several businesses exist), but the **€19.95–30.90 band is the market price** for a
  single beginner kit; €34–40 exists only at Willy Wolle (small).
- **The planned "differentiators" are table stakes**: German video, pre-started first round, easy-to-see yarn, gift box and
  personal help are already offered by Woobles, Die Bobbels, Willy Wolle and even Chinese Amazon sellers.
- **No dominant German player** was found — the German specialists look small (GbR / ~1k followers). Possible reading:
  (a) room for a better marketer, or (b) the paid-acquisition economics are hard and nobody has scaled. Both fit the evidence.
- Not verified (blocked): traffic, ad activity (Meta Ad Library), Etsy shop sales, any revenue figure for DE players.
