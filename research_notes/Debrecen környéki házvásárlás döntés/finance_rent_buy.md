## 1. Mortgage market and subsidy programs (Otthon Start, CSOK Plusz, Falusi CSOK, Babaváró, renovation grants, market rates, MNB rules)

### Takeaway
Otthon Start (fixed 3%, up to 50 M HUF, 25 years, 10% down) is still available in Oct 2026. It has no statutory end date and covers used family houses in villages. Since 1 Jan 2026 it also covers külterület/zártkert properties. Its main risk is a tightening under review (a possible age cap of about 35–40, a ban on renting out). Market 10-year-fixed mortgages cost about 6.2–6.9% interest, or 6.2–7.3% THM. The energetic home-renovation grants are closed: suspended on 23 Apr 2026, and the rural program ended on 30 Jun 2026.

### Cited Findings

**Otthon Start: core parameters** (legal basis: Gov. Decree 227/2025)
- Fixed 3% interest for the whole term. Max loan 50 M HUF, max term 25 years, min down payment 10%. **VERIFIED** (consistent across several sources) — [Bankmonitor Otthon Start page](https://bankmonitor.hu/otthon-start-hitel/); [Banknavigator](https://banknavigator.hu/otthon-start-hitel/); [OTP Bank Otthon Start page](https://www.otpbank.hu/portal/hu/lakashitel/lakastamogatasok/otthon-start-program); [money.hu: final decree published, 1 Aug 2025](https://tudastar.money.hu/hir/20250801/megjelent-a-vegleges-jogszabaly-az-otthon-start-fix-3-os-hitelhez-most-mar-minden-reszlet-ismert/)
- Price caps: apartment market value ≤100 M HUF; **family house ≤150 M HUF**; in both cases the bank valuer's value must be ≤1.5 M HUF/m². **VERIFIED** — [e-ingatlanugyvedek, 2026](https://e-ingatlanugyvedek.hu/blog/3-szazalekos-hitel-elso-lakasra); [Banknavigator](https://banknavigator.hu/otthon-start-hitel/)
- From 1 Jan 2026 Otthon Start can also be used for **külterületi and zártkerti** residential properties. **LIKELY** — [search summary of e-ingatlanugyvedek / bankmonitor](https://e-ingatlanugyvedek.hu/blog/3-szazalekos-hitel-elso-lakasra); [biztosdontes: Otthon Start külterületen](https://biztosdontes.hu/cikkek/otthon-start-kulteruleten-is-mire-figyelj-ha-videki-ingatlanban-gondolkodsz)
- Used homes are eligible. About 90% of Otthon Start loans finance used dwellings, and the average loan is about 35 M HUF (MNB). **VERIFIED** (MNB report) — [MNB Lakáspiaci jelentés, May 2026](https://www.mnb.hu/letoltes/laka-spiaci-jelente-s-2026-ma-jus.pdf)
- Property-ownership eligibility: in the 10 years before applying, the applicant may have held **at most one inner-area (belterületi) residential property, with at most a 50% share**. A share above 50% is allowed only if that share's value is/was ≤15 M HUF. **LIKELY** (search summary of several articles; check the exact wording in the decree) — [Baon, Aug 2025](https://www.baon.hu/helyi-gazdasag/2025/08/otthon-start-program-lakashitel-tulajdonjog); [Bankmonitor: final decree](https://bankmonitor.hu/mediatar/cikk/otthon-start-megjelent-a-vegleges-jogszabaly/)
- Personal conditions: age 18+, at least **2 years of continuous TB (social security) status**, with gaps of up to 30 days allowed; clean criminal record; no public debts. **LIKELY** — [Banknavigator: TB & büntetlen előélet](https://banknavigator.hu/otthon-start-hitel-a-buntetlen-eloelet-fontossaga-es-a-tb-jogviszony-feltetelei/); [Bankmonitor: final decree](https://bankmonitor.hu/mediatar/cikk/otthon-start-megjelent-a-vegleges-jogszabaly/)

- Deadline: the decree has **no fixed end date ("visszavonásig")**. The loan application must be submitted within 180 days of the final sale contract. Individual banks set their own campaign deadlines: Raiffeisen accepted applications until 30 Sep 2026, with loan promises until 31 Dec 2026. **LIKELY** — [okosdontes: Otthon Start 2026 mikor jár le](https://okosdontes.hu/piaci-korkep/otthon-start-program-2026-mikor-jar-le-es-erdemes-most-belevagni/); [e-ingatlanugyvedek: meddig tart](https://e-ingatlanugyvedek.hu/blog/az-otthon-start-program-meddig-tart); [UniCredit product sheet 2026.02.01](https://www.unicreditbank.hu/content/dam/cee2020-pws-hu/Maganszemelyek/Hitelek/2026/202602/Term%C3%A9kismertet%C5%91_Otthon%20Start_2026.02.01.pdf)

**CSOK Plusz** (married couples taking on a new child)
- Amounts: 15 M HUF with 1 child taken on; 30 M HUF with 2 children (at least 1 taken on); 50 M HUF with 3 or more children (at least 1 taken on). Minimum 2.5 M HUF. Interest at most 3%, term 10–25 years. The wife must be under 41 (or pregnant if older). At least one child must be taken on (vállalás); a childless couple that does not commit to a child is not eligible. From the 2nd (new) child onward, 10 M HUF of the debt is written off per child. **LIKELY** — [Bankmonitor CSOK Plusz 2026](https://bankmonitor.hu/csok-feltetelei/); [money.hu CSOK Plusz 2026](https://www.money.hu/csok-plusz)

**Falusi CSOK**
- The current law has **no final application deadline**; it runs until withdrawn. **Babaváró**
- 11 M HUF, interest-free, freely usable, max term 20 years, for married couples. Since 1 Jan 2025 the **wife must be under 35** (raised from 30); pregnancy no longer exempts older couples. At least 3 years of continuous TB status. If no child is born within 5 years, the interest subsidy must be repaid. **LIKELY** — [Bankmonitor: Babaváró 2025 korhatár-emelés](https://bankmonitor.hu/mediatar/cikk/babavaro-2025-az-erdeklodok-30-a-orulhet-a-korhatar-emelesnek/); [Bankmonitor Babaváró 2026](https://bankmonitor.hu/babavaro-hitel/); [Portfolio, 11 Jan 2026](https://www.portfolio.hu/bank/20260111/babavaro-hitel-2026-feltetelek-igenyles-visszafizetes-tudnivalok-es-valtozasok-egy-helyen-810097)

**MNB borrower-based rules (HFM/JTM; FAQ version effective 27 Feb 2026)**
- **HFM (LTV):** 80% general limit. **90% for first-time buyers** since 1 Jan 2025 (allows a 10% down payment). The 90% limit does not apply if any co-borrower ever owned a residential share of 50% or more. **LIKELY** — [money.hu: 10 vagy 20% önerő](https://tudastar.money.hu/ismerteto/lakashitel-10-vagy-20-szazalek-onero/); [MNB adósságfék page](https://www.mnb.hu/penzugyi-stabilitas/makroprudencialis-politika/makroprudencialis-eszkoztar/adossagfek-szabalyok-hfm-jtm)
- **JTM (DSTI):** for net household income **below 800k HUF/month**, the limit is 25% (fixation under 5 years), 35% (5–10 years), or **50% (10 years or longer, incl. fixed-for-term)**. At or above 800k it is 30% / 40% / 60%. Green loans: 60% regardless of income. **LIKELY** — [MNB FAQ, version from 27 Feb 2026](https://www.mnb.hu/penzugyi-stabilitas/makroprudencialis-politika/makroprudencialis-eszkoztar/adossagfek-szabalyok-hfm-jtm/gyakran-ismetelt-kerdesek); [e-ingatlanugyvedek JTM](https://e-ingatlanugyvedek.hu/jtm-es-hitelbiztositeki-ertek-szamitasa); [berkalkulator JTM 2026](https://berkalkulator.com/blog/2026/lakashitel-jtm-kalkulator-2026-utmutato)

### Inferences
- **The couple's likely best route is Otthon Start.** A 20–35 M HUF village house is far below the 150 M HUF / 1.5 M HUF/m² caps. They can use it if: (a) neither has owned more than one inner-area home share above 50% (or above 15 M HUF) in the past 10 years; (b) both have at least 2 years of continuous TB status; (c) the rules do not change before the bank accepts the application. ESTIMATE.
- **Timing risk favors acting sooner.** Under review are an age cap (35–40) and other conditions. Couples in their late 30s are the ones at risk. Banks' own campaign deadlines (Raiffeisen 30 Sep 2026) suggest some lenders are cautious. Waiting 1–3 years risks losing the 3% rate, which is worth roughly 30–45k HUF/month on a 20 M HUF loan (see Section 2). ESTIMATE.
- The JTM limit is unlikely to bind for Otthon Start. At 3%, a 25 M HUF / 25-year loan costs about 118.6k/month, so a 50% JTM needs only about 237k HUF net household income. At a market rate of 6.9%, the same loan (about 175k) needs about 350k net. ESTIMATE (from the Section 2 tables).
- If they plan children: CSOK Plusz (wife under 41) can be combined with Otthon Start and makes the purchase illeték-exempt. Babaváró (wife under 35) can fund the down payment or renovation. Falusi CSOK may add a grant if the village is on the list. Ages are unknown, so model the subsidy scenarios separately.

## 2. Monthly instalment tables (annuity math)

### Takeaway
At 3% (Otthon Start), each 10 M HUF borrowed costs about 47.4k/month over 25 years or 55.5k over 20 years. At about 6.2–6.9% (market 10-year fixed), it costs about 65.7–70.0k (25 years) or 72.8–76.9k (20 years). The subsidy saves roughly 35–40% of the monthly payment.

### Cited Findings
- Formula used: M = P·r / (1 − (1+r)^−n), with r = annual rate / 12 and n = years × 12. The remaining balance after k months is B = P(1+r)^k − M[(1+r)^k − 1]/r. Rate inputs: 3% Otthon Start ([Bankmonitor](https://bankmonitor.hu/otthon-start-hitel/)); 6.2% and 6.9% as the low and high ends of September 2026 10-year-fixed offers ([Bankmonitor September](https://bankmonitor.hu/mediatar/cikk/ezek-a-legkedvezobb-lakashitel-ajanlatok-szeptemberben/)). The results are **ESTIMATES**: they use the nominal rate, so THM-related fees and insurance are not included.

### Inferences
- **The 3% subsidy is worth about 4–4.5 M HUF of interest over the first 5 years, and 7.5–8 M over 10 years, on a 20–25 M HUF loan, compared with a market rate of about 6.5%.** This is the single largest money factor in "buy now vs. wait". It only matters if the couple would lose access to Otthon Start by waiting (age cap or program change).
- In the first 5 years of a 3% / 25-year loan, roughly half of each payment reduces principal (about 0.29 M of each 0.57 M paid per 10 M borrowed). At 6.9%, only about a quarter does.

### Gaps
- THM figures include fees and mandatory insurance. Real THM for Otthon Start is typically slightly above 3%, but exact per-bank THM was not retrieved (UNKNOWN).

## 3. Purchase transaction costs

### Takeaway
For a 20–35 M HUF used house bought with Otthon Start alone, budget about **5–6% of the price** in transaction costs. Most of it is the 4% illeték (0.8–1.4 M HUF), which disappears if CSOK Plusz or Falusi CSOK is combined. Lawyer fees run about 0.4–0.6% in the countryside, with a 100–150k minimum.

### Cited Findings
- Vagyonszerzési illeték: 4% of market value. First-time buyers under 35 get a 50% reduction (2% instead of 4%) **only if the property value is ≤15 M HUF**. New-build homes are exempt up to 15 M HUF; between 15 and 30 M HUF, 4% is due only on the part above 15 M. A CSOK-financed purchase is fully exempt with no value cap. **LIKELY** — [money.hu illetékkedvezmény](https://tudastar.money.hu/fogalom/illetekkedvezmeny/); [financera: vagyonszerzési illeték 2026](https://financera.hu/szemelyes-penzugyek/vagyonszerzesi-illetek/); [bank360 CSOK illetékmentesség](https://bank360.hu/blog/csok-illetekmentesseg)
- Otthon Start alone is **not** illeték-exempt; combining it with CSOK Plusz or Falusi CSOK makes the purchase exempt. **LIKELY** — [szendreiadam.hu](https://szendreiadam.hu/hitel/otthon-start-3-lakashitel-feltetelek/)
- Lawyer fee (usually paid by the buyer): typically 0.3–1% of the price; **0.4–0.6% in the countryside**; minimum about 100,000–150,000 HUF. **LIKELY** — [money.hu: ingatlanvásárlás költségei 2026](https://tudastar.money.hu/ismerteto/az-ingatlanvasarlas-koltsegei-ezekkel-a-koltsegekkel-szamolhatunk-a-vetelaron-felul/); [financera](https://financera.hu/hitelek/ingatlan-vasarlas-koltsegei/)
