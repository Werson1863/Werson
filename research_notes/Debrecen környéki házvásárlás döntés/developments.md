## 1. Industrial developments (BMW, CATL, EVE, Semcorp, EcoPro, others; industrial zones; airport/cargo)

### Takeaway
Debrecen's industrial jobs growth is real and documented in 2026, and it splits into two zones:
- **North-west (Északnyugati Gazdasági Övezet, around Józsa / Kismacs / road 35 / M35):** BMW and EVE Power.
- **South (Déli Gazdasági Övezet, along road 47 toward Mikepércs and Sáránd):** CATL, Semcorp, the CATL subsidiary GreenPower and other plants.

BMW is fully ramped up: more than 5,000 staff and three shifts since September 2026. CATL began trial production in September 2026 after delays. Semcorp is shut down by the authorities. EcoPro has been in mass production since May 2026. EVE Power is targeting series production in the second half of 2027.

### Inferences
- The two job clusters sit on opposite sides of the city. The NW zone (BMW, EVE) serves Józsa, Hajdúböszörmény, Balmazújváros, Hajdúsámson and Nyíradony via the 35 road, M35 and 471 road. The south zone (CATL, EcoPro?, Semcorp, GreenPower) serves Mikepércs, Sáránd, Ebes, Hajdúszoboszló, Derecske and Hosszúpályi via the 47 and 4 roads and the 481 road.
- Commuting-time access to these zones is likely to support demand in the suburban ring. The settlements right next to the battery plants (Mikepércs, Sáránd, Debrecen-Józsa's edge) also carry nuisance and reputational risk (see section 5).
- BMW is the most solid and least controversial employer: it is running at full speed with more than 5,000 jobs. The battery cluster is much more uncertain:
  - Semcorp is shut down.
  - CATL's second unit is undecided.
  - The new government is reviewing major battery investments (section 6).

## 2. Roads (M4, 4-es főút, 481 southern bypass, eastern bypass, 33/47/48/471, M35)

### Takeaway
Almost every major Debrecen-area road project is still in planning or paperwork. None of the large new ones is under construction as of October 2026.
- The 481 southern bypass 4-laning has a permit, but no construction contract.
- The eastern bypass has an environmental permit and execution design.
- The Debrecen–Hajdúszoboszló 4-laning of the 4-es was struck off the implementation list in March 2026.
- The Debrecen–Nyíregyháza 2x2 road is only in early design.
- The M4 toward Püspökladány is not expected before 2029–2032.

The new government's road priorities for Debrecen are publicly unclear.
