## Which settlements are clearly stronger (affluent, growing, suburbanising) vs. clearly weaker (declining, high unemployment, disadvantaged areas, high crime)?

### Takeaway
The split is clear. The **inner suburban ring north and west of Debrecen is growing**: Bocskaikert +25%, Mikepércs +17–19%, Hajdúsámson +7–11% and Ebes +4% (2011→2022 census). The spa and market towns **Hajdúszoboszló (+2%)** and, to a lesser extent, Hajdúböszörmény, Hajdúnánás and Balmazújváros **rank high on the Egyensúly Intézet 2026 development index**. The **east and south-east (Nyíradonyi, Derecskei and Berettyóújfalui járás) and the Sárrét (Püspökladányi járás)** sit in "komplex programmal fejlesztendő" districts. Many of their villages are on the kedvezményezett list, and towns like Derecske (−6.7%) and Berettyóújfalu (−6/−7.6%) lose population. **Hajdúhadház** (reported as the 3rd-least-developed small town in Hungary) and **Hosszúpályi** (4th-least-developed village) are the clearest weak outliers. I could not obtain settlement-level unemployment, SZJA-base and crime-per-1000 figures, so those columns remain gaps.

### Inferences
- **Stronger (data-backed):** Bocskaikert, Mikepércs and Hajdúsámson are growing fast, i.e. suburbanising. Ebes is growing with a high index rank. Hajdúszoboszló has a top-15 index rank and slight growth. **Middle:** Hajdúböszörmény, Hajdúnánás and Balmazújváros rank well but are losing 3–6% of their population. **Weaker:** Hajdúhadház and Hosszúpályi (bottom of national index categories). The Nyíradonyi-district villages and Derecske, Pocsaj and Sáránd fall in KP districts and/or on the kedvezményezett list. Berettyóújfalu and Püspökladány are central towns of KP districts that are losing population, though their index ranks are mid-table.
- The growth ring and the deprivation pockets sit next to each other. Bocskaikert (+25%) lies beside Hajdúhadház (bottom-3 small town), and Mikepércs (+17%) is near Hosszúpályi (bottom-4 village). A buyer should judge at street/neighbourhood level, not just by settlement.
- Hajdúhadházi RK crime fell 18% in 2025. With no population base for the RK area, I cannot compute crimes per 1,000. ESTIMATE only: if the area held ~30–35k people (Hajdúhadház + Téglás + Bocskaikert; UNVERIFIED-BACKGROUND), 588 crimes would mean ~17–20 per 1,000. That is above the county's ~9.4 per 1,000, but this is a rough estimate.

## Which settlements have notable industrial zones, main roads through residential areas, or rail lines that create noise?

### Takeaway
The documented hotspot is **Debrecen's Southern Economic Zone (Déli Gazdasági Övezet)**, home to the CATL battery plant and the EcoPro cathode plant. **Mikepércs is the closest settlement.** Its residents and civil groups report noise ("humming") and pollution concerns, and in 2023 Mikepércs, Sáránd and Hosszúpályi saw crowded guest-worker housing in family homes. A further Korean cathode plant was under public hearing in Oct 2026. I could not verify road and rail noise exposure from sources this session.

### Cited Findings
- **Mikepércs is the settlement closest to the CATL battery plant** in Debrecen's southern industrial zone; the industrial park is visible from the edge of the settlement. LIKELY (2022–24) — [HAON 2022-12, Mikepércs monitoring station](https://www.haon.hu/helyi-kozelet/2022/12/mikepercs-monitoring-halozatot-fog-epiteni); [hang.hu, Mikepércs protest](https://hang.hu/belfold/mikepercsi-civilek-tuntettek-a-kozseg-hataraba-tervezett-akkugyar-ellen-152904)
