# Debrecen-area job market (2026) for a logistics engineer / Lean process engineer candidate

**Research context and method limits (read first).** Research date: 2026-10-07. Every page fetch (profession.hu, hu.indeed.com, haon.hu, telex.hu, hrportal.hu, vg.hu, careers.dhl.com, the Hays PDF) was blocked by the network egress proxy. All findings therefore come from **web search result titles and snippets** (search-engine summaries of those pages). Then the shared web-search budget ran out (200 calls per turn) before planned searches on BMW job IDs, Debrecen hotels, the 2026 minimum wage and the latest CATL news. Consequences:
- The URLs below are real: each one came back as a search result. The posting details are snippet-level. I could not open any posting to confirm that it is still live, its exact date or its full salary text.
- Labels: **VERIFIED** = a search result shows the stated fact directly and the date is clear. **LIKELY** = it comes from a snippet but the date or the attribution is a little uncertain. **ESTIMATE** = my own inference. **UNKNOWN** = not found.
- Profession.hu posting IDs rise over time. IDs around 3,000,000–3,021,000 appear on listing pages labelled Aug–Oct 2026. IDs around 2,81x,xxx–2,88x,xxx are older (probably 2025) and **may have expired**. This dating is an ESTIMATE based on the ID order.

## Q1. Concrete current/recent job postings (Debrecen + nearby)

### Takeaway
I found around 35 concrete postings or recruiting signals that fit the profile. They cluster around the EV and automotive build-out: CATL (CATH), EVE Power, BMW, DHL Supply Chain (the BMW project), ZF, Krones, thyssenkrupp, Teva, Deufol and staffing agencies (Trenkwalder). Good-fit roles are logistics engineer, logistics SAP key user, procurement, production/supply-chain planning and process engineering. Many ask for 1–3 years of experience and fluent or intermediate English, which fits B2. Most postings state no salary. Where a salary is stated, white-collar logistics, procurement and quality roles in Debrecen sit at about **500–800k HUF gross/month**. Power Platform/RPA developer roles are at **1.0–2.0M HUF** but need 3–5 years or more of experience.

### Takeaway
A realistic **gross** monthly range for a logistics engineer with some (not senior) experience in Debrecen is **~550–800k HUF**. Entry or junior specialist roles start around 500k. Planner, process or lean roles at large multinationals (BMW, CATL, Krones) can reach **800k–1M HUF** with experience. Hays (national, Budapest-weighted) figures are higher than Profession.hu's Hajdú-Bihar asking-wage data. Admin and hospitality fallbacks pay roughly **300–600k gross**. The Hajdú-Bihar average gross wage in H1 2026 was **675,733 HUF**.

## Q4. Labour market trends 2025–2026 (ramp-ups, freezes, layoffs)

### Takeaway
Debrecen bucks the national automotive downturn. BMW (+2,000 jobs in one year, 3 shifts), CATL (~2,900 employees, cell pilot production since Sept 2026) and EVE Power (400 hires for 2027 SOP) are all expanding. Nationally, 17 of the 25 largest automotive employers cut headcount in the year to July 2026, and a factory-closure wave is under way. The local risks are CATL's slower-than-announced ramp and selective cuts at suppliers (thyssenkrupp development jobs).

### Cited Findings
- Of the 25 largest automotive companies in Hungary, **17 cut headcount** between July 2025 and July 2026. BMW, Mercedes and BYD hiring masks the decline: VERIFIED (snippet) — [Telex G7, 2026-09-22](https://telex.hu/g7/vallalat/2026/09/22/nagy-autoipari-uzem-letszam-bmw-byd-mercedes-leepites-wkw); [autopro.hu](https://autopro.hu/hr/a-bmw-es-a-mercedes-elfedi-a-magyar-jarmuipari-leepiteseket/1685046)

## Q6. Where the employers are (industrial zones, for commute mapping)

### Takeaway
Two zones dominate. The **Southern Economic Zone (DGÖ)** next to Debrecen airport, south of the city, hosts CATL, Krones, Deufol and Schaeffler (ex-Vitesco). The **Northwestern Economic Zone (ÉNYGÖ)**, north-west of the city, hosts BMW, its supplier park, ZF, Schedl and EVE Power. Office and IT jobs (DT IT Solutions) are in the city at Tudáspark.

### Cited Findings
- **DGÖ** (Déli Gazdasági Övezet, Southern Economic Zone): 710 ha next to Debrecen International Airport, with a 16 km internal road network and three SME parks. Tenants: **CATL** (221 ha), **Krones**, **Deufol**, **Vitesco/Schaeffler**: VERIFIED (snippet) — [haon 2024-10](https://www.haon.hu/helyi-gazdasag/2024/10/deli-gazdasagi-ovezet-debrecen-catl); [VG 2023-08](https://www.vg.hu/vilaggazdasag-magyar-gazdasag/2023/08/hatvanmilliardbol-fejleszt-debrecen-a-bmw-leendo-otthona-is-megszepul)
- **ÉNYGÖ** (Észak-Nyugati Gazdasági Övezet, Northwestern Economic Zone): 620 ha, of which 400 ha is the **BMW** site and 100 ha the **supplier park**. Tenants: **ZF**, **Schedl Group**, **EVE Power** (EVE is "directly near the BMW plant"): VERIFIED (snippet) — [DIF Debrecen – Northwestern Economic Zone](https://dif.debrecen.hu/en/northwestern-economic-zone/); [Debreceni Nap 2026-01-20](https://debreceninap.hu/helyi/2026/01/20/400-uj-munkavallalot-venne-fel-a-debreceni-eve-power-akkumulatorgyar/)
