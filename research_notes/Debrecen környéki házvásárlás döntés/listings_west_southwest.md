# Family-house listings and local markets W/SW of Debrecen (October 2026)

**How this was researched (read first):**
- **Access limits:** WebFetch and curl were **blocked by the network egress policy** for every property site tried: ingatlan.com, megveszlak.hu, koltozzbe.hu, ingatlantajolo.hu, ingatlanmaps.hu, ingatlanok.hu, oc.hu, dh.hu, ingatlan.jofogas.hu, teleingatlan.hu and haon.hu. **No listing page was opened directly.** Every data point below comes from **search-engine result titles and snippets**, which a search tool summarised. The web-search budget ran out partway through, so some planned lookups were not done (Ebes Alkotmány u. details, Mikepércs 34.9 M details, Hajdúszoboszló ≤35 M listings, Hortobágy, Szerep listings, distances and commute times).
- **Labels used:**
  - **VERIFIED** = the indexed page title states the fact (for example an ingatlan.com title with street and ID).
  - **LIKELY** = the fact appears in a snippet summary tied to a named page, but the page was not opened.
  - **ESTIMATE** = my own derivation.
  - **UNKNOWN** = not found.
- **Whether a listing is still active in October 2026 is UNKNOWN for every listing.** Search indexes can hold sold or expired ads. Before any visit, check each URL in a normal browser.
- Asking prices are not transaction prices. The HUF/m² averages from portals mix flats and houses and all conditions.

## 1. Settlement-level prices, supply and HUF/m²

### Takeaway
The settlements fall into a clear price gradient:
- Debrecen: about 1.05–1.11 M HUF/m².
- Hajdúszoboszló: about 0.92 M.
- Ebes: about 0.86 M.
- Mikepércs: about 0.78–0.79 M.
- Balmazújváros: about 0.74 M.
- Nagyhegyes: about 0.71 M.
- Kaba: about 0.41 M.
- Hajdúszovát (~0.32 M), Püspökladány (~0.28 M) and Nádudvar (~0.25 M): data partly from 2025.
- Sárrét villages: lower still. Szerep is an extreme low.

With 30 M HUF, the Debrecen agglomeration ring (Ebes, Mikepércs, Hajdúszoboszló) buys only about 33–40 m² at average prices. Kaba, Hajdúszovát, Nádudvar and Püspökladány are where a habitable house of 75 m² or more fits the budget.

## 3. Price premiums (Hajdúszoboszló, Ebes/Mikepércs), liquidity and hard-to-resell settlements

### Takeaway
- **Hajdúszoboszló** is the most expensive settlement in the area apart from Debrecen itself (about 919k/m²). This reflects the spa and tourism market, including the flat and holiday-let segment. It is the only one where a −2% six-month drift was reported.
- **Ebes and Mikepércs** are strongly suburbanising: about 858k and 783k/m², and haon reports Ebes houses selling in 2 weeks to 1 month.
- **Szerep and deep-Sárrét villages** are cheap but thin and slow to resell.

### Inferences
- **Liquidity tiers (ESTIMATE):**
  1. Ebes, Mikepércs, Hajdúszoboszló: fast resale, but entry is out of budget for a good 3-room house.
  2. Balmazújváros, Nagyhegyes, Hajdúszovát: medium. Thin supply; Nagyhegyes has about 5–20 ads.
  3. Kaba, Nádudvar, Püspökladány: towns with services and rail line 100 (Kaba, Püspökladány). Medium liquidity and affordable.
  4. Báránd, Sárrétudvari, Tetétlen, Földes, Szerep: cheap, thin and slow. These have **resale risk** and an older stock with more vályog. Szerep and Sárrétudvari stand out on price level and enforcement presence.
- The Hajdúszoboszló premium is partly a flat and holiday-let effect. Family houses on the town's edges may be closer to 600–800k/m², but this is unverified.
- Hajdúszovát and Nagyhegyes sit between Debrecen and Hajdúszoboszló and are not yet priced like Ebes. They are the most likely places for "suburbanisation spillover" upside, but supply is very thin (ESTIMATE).
